          Shortcuts & help

          Quick ways to use the family tree.

          • / search a name   F fit view   + / − zoom
          • Search works in Hindi or English. Type names like bhawar singh or raam and the Hindi names will show in the results.
          • The bell shows new blog posts and updates (a red dot means something new). The lens searches, and the three-line menu opens Home, Updates, Blog, Download, Request, Statistics, Help and About. The sun / moon button beside the bell switches day and night mode.
          • The slider icon at the bottom left opens the tree tools and they close by themselves when not used. Tap a tool to see its name. At the bottom right: zoom in, zoom out and fit to screen.
          • Click a name to open or close its branch. The details card appears only when you press the ⓘ badge next to a name, or pick someone from search. Use the Click button to choose one generation or the full branch.
          • Copy link in the details card shares a direct link to that person.
          • Mark as person A, then open another person to see how the two are related.
          • Menu → Download saves the visible tree as an image, SVG or PDF.
          • Menu → Request sends your request to the record keeper by email.
